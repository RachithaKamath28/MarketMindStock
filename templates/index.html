📉 Residual Plot

Each dot is one forecast: its horizontal position is the predicted price, its vertical position is how far off it was (actual − predicted). Dots scattered evenly around the zero line mean no systematic bias.

📊 Prediction Error Distribution

How often forecast errors of each size occurred. A tall peak near zero, with few large errors on either side, indicates a consistent model.
